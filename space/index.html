Português → Mirandés

Mirandese (lhéngua mirandesa) is an Astur-Leonese language of the Terra de Miranda, in north-east Portugal. This page translates European Portuguese into Mirandese with hand-written rules from the Convenção Ortográfica da Língua Mirandesa (1999) and word lists learned from Wikipedia, Wiktionary and the Lhenguabase. Where the corpus and the Convenção differ, it follows the Convenção.

It is not a native translation: no speaker has reviewed the output, and rules miss idioms and words they have never seen. Each line is translated on its own, in your browser.
